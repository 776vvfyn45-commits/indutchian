Our Story

One name. Two cultures. Endless flavour.

Founded by Faye & Rohith, Indutchian is what happens when India and the Netherlands sit at the same table. The name says it all, In + Dutch + ian, India and the Netherlands in one word, and so does the food: the warmth and spice of India meeting the comfort and craft of Dutch cooking.

We don't just put two cuisines side by side, we blend them until they understand each other. Every dish is a little conversation between two kitchens that turned out to have a lot in common.

The Indian Side

Warmth, spice, and colour. Bold flavours, slow-cooked love, and a table that's always open.

The Dutch Side

Comfort, craft, and clean simplicity. Honest ingredients and the cosy feeling of gezellig.
